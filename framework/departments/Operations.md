# Operations

> The one department the framework installs, because every organisation has it whether or not it has a
> name for it: the work of keeping the work honest. **Rename it, split it, or delete it** once your real
> departments exist — it is a worked example, not a claim about how you should be organised.

## What it owns

The workflows, the weekly rhythm and the standard that a written thing has to meet before anyone relies
on it. In a small organisation this is the founder's own overhead, and naming it as a department is what
makes it possible to hand any of it over later.

## Who leads it

The **Operations Executive** — `01 - Company/Roles/Operations Executive.md`, compiled into the agent
`operations-executive`. It recommends; it does not decide.

## How you know it is working

Somebody back from a week away opens `05 - Operations/picture/ai_operations.html` and knows what happened, what
is waiting and what is stale — without asking anyone. If they have to ask, this department is not working
yet, whatever the page says.
