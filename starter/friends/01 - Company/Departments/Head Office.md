# Head Office

> Where the organisation decides things and keeps track of itself. In a company this is the founder's
> own overhead; for one person it is the part of the week that is not doing the work. It ships with the
> framework because every other department needs somewhere to escalate to.

## What it owns

Decisions, the weekly rhythm, and the map of who answers for what. It does not do the work — it knows
where the work is and who is accountable for it.

## Where it stops

At the work itself, and at the machinery. Ask Head Office how a client project is going and it should
hand you to that department. Ask it why the page says nine when there are seven and it should hand you
to AI Operations.
