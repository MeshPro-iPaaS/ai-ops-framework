# AI Operations

> The department that keeps the rest of it honest. It owns nothing anybody sells and nothing anybody
> delivers — it owns the fact that everything written down is still true, and that the agents reading
> it are reading the current version.

## What it owns

The machinery: the templates notes are made from, the generators that turn notes into agents and pages,
the checks that fail when a surface would state something untrue, and the site itself. Without this
department the vault becomes a folder of stale documents in about three weeks, and nobody notices.

## Where it stops

At content. AI Operations makes sure a note is readable, owned, and not contradicted somewhere else.
Whether the note says the right thing is the department the note belongs to.
