# {{title}}

> One paragraph: what this specialist is for, and when its executive should reach for it.

A specialist is narrow on purpose. It does one job properly and hands everything else back to the
executive it reports to. If you find yourself widening it, you wanted a second specialist.

## What it never does

The `not_owned` list above, in prose. A specialist that answers outside its one job is worse than no
specialist, because the answer looks as confident as the good ones.
